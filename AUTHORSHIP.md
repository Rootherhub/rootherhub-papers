# Authorship and AI disclosure

Jair Ibarra (ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0008-8796-8888) is the author of every paper in this registry. The arguments are his. The professional experience each paper draws on, the projects, materials, documents and failures described, is his own, from his practice as an architect, contractor, consultant and builder of decision systems. The judgment about what to say and the final voice are his.

AI tools are used in producing these papers, and the use is disclosed here rather than hidden. In this practice they work as editorial instruments: verifying claims against sources, running an adversarial edit that challenges every figure and every convenient sentence, structuring drafts, and keeping the versioned record of how each text changed. They generate volume; the author curates, marks the seam, and finishes by hand. No interview, project, quote, statistic, or credential in any paper is invented. Every factual claim is traceable to a checked source in the private editorial record.

Each paper is fingerprinted (SHA-256 of the exact approved file) and registered in this repository at the moment it is completed. The commit timestamp and the fingerprint together establish that the complete text existed in that form on that date. Amendments are recorded openly: the earlier fingerprint is retained in the entry and in git history.
